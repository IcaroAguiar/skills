# Explore

Use only while the governing direction is genuinely undecided.

Produce at most two candidates. They must differ in posture, governing
relationship, composition, component strategy, asset role, and behavior—not
only color or styling.

For each candidate state:

```text
Posture:
Thesis:
Dominant composition:
Signature:
Contextual components:
Critical assets:
Platform behavior:
Strength:
Cost and risk:
Reason to reject:
```

Recommend one. Name what must not be combined between the candidates. Continue
with `execute`; exploration alone is not delivery.
