# Audit

Determine whether the rendered interface is styled, composed, authored,
refined, or premium.

Inspect:

- context specificity;
- visible thesis;
- dominant composition and attention path;
- semantic visual-system roles;
- task-specific component choices;
- assets and fallbacks;
- signature usefulness;
- state and multi-screen coherence;
- motion and feedback;
- platform adaptation;
- content resilience;
- design-direction revalidation;
- evidence supporting the claimed level.

Use [audit-report.md](../templates/audit-report.md). Treat an attractive
screenshot with unverified behavior as static craft evidence, not proof of
premium or native quality.
