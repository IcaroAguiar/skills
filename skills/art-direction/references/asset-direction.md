# Asset Direction

Treat assets as structural parts of the direction when they establish identity,
subject, proof, instruction, data meaning, or narrative.

Inventory:

```text
Asset:
Class: structural | brand | contextual | decorative
Function:
Placement:
Visual direction:
Required variants:
Fallback:
Genericity risk:
Production method:
```

Potential assets include photography, portraits, product art, illustrations,
domain icons, graphic motifs, textures, data representations, editorial image,
empty states, motion assets, sound, and semantic haptics.

When a critical asset does not exist:

1. name the gap and its function;
2. direct the asset precisely;
3. generate, find, or request it when authorized and tools allow;
4. define a fallback that preserves hierarchy;
5. never compensate with extra cards, shadows, gradients, or generic stock art.

Decorative assets remain optional and subordinate.
