# Motion and Feedback

Specify every material transition:

```text
Event:
Previous state:
Transition:
Next state:
Relative duration:
Purpose:
Visual feedback:
Haptic or sound:
Accessible feedback:
Reduced-motion behavior:
Interruption:
Failure or undo:
```

Use motion to explain causality, continuity, depth, return, selection, state
change, completion, or reorganization.

Avoid ceremonial entry motion in frequent work, inconsistent transitions,
blocking sequences, motion-only state, decorative parallax, success modals for
routine completion, and haptics on every touch.

The motion plan is required even when the deliverable is static. A plan is not
runtime proof.
