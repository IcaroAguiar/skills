# Product Context

Classify before choosing an aesthetic.

```text
Product:
Domain:
Users:
Dominant task:
Frequency:
Consequence of error:
Information density:
Trust requirement:
Brand posture:
Expression budget:
Platform:
Physical context:
Expected visual maturity:
```

Translate each answer into a constraint. Frequent, consequential work generally
needs quieter expression, faster scan, stable placement, and restrained motion.
Discovery, identity, or emotional consumption may support stronger image,
material, or spatial authorship.

Do not reuse a direction across health, finance, entertainment, education, and
internal tools without showing why their constraints converge.
