# Mobile Art Direction

The screen is the primary surface. Do not distribute web cards and controls
inside a narrow viewport.

## Preserve

Preserve product concept, posture, terminology, color meaning, content voice,
and signature relationship.

## Adapt

Adapt navigation and back behavior, sheets and dialogs, bars and safe areas,
gestures and touch response, system materials, motion and haptics, keyboard and
focus, interruption, restoration, density, and action geography.

Consistency of product does not require interaction uniformity across iOS and
Android.

## Execute decisions

Declare:

```text
Task-first composition:
Navigation topology:
Primary scroll owner:
Persistent action behavior:
Keyboard and inset response:
Touch targets and pressed states:
System versus custom components:
Gesture alternatives:
Dynamic content response:
iOS differences:
Android differences:
```

Prefer established platform patterns for navigation, input, selection,
confirmation, destructive action, sharing, files, and permissions. Custom
components require a functional or brand advantage plus complete touch,
semantics, focus, text scaling, localization, alternate input, reduced motion,
and platform behavior.

Do not claim native or premium without applicable runtime proof. A mobile-web
render can demonstrate authored composition and refined static craft only.
