# Web Art Direction

Preserve the surface mode:

- operational: scan, compare, change state, act safely;
- analytical: ask, filter, compare evidence, intervene;
- transactional: understand consequence and complete with confidence;
- editorial: follow voice, measure, sequence, and comprehension;
- persuasive: understand proposition, proof, and conversion.

Do not import landing-page grammar into an operational workspace.

## Execute decisions

Declare:

```text
Grid and dominant axis:
Persistent chrome:
Primary content plane:
Selection and detail relationship:
Action geography:
Pointer and keyboard behavior:
What reflows:
What reveals:
What changes presentation:
What becomes contextual:
```

Use familiar web components when they reduce learning cost, then integrate
their type, density, focus, state, material, and motion into the product.

Customize only for clearer relationships, faster recognition, stronger
continuity, safer decisions, or meaningful brand expression.

Responsive work must transform presentation, not merely collapse columns.
