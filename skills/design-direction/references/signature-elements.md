# Signature Elements

Choose at most one primary signature and one supporting device.

A signature may be:

- a compositional axis;
- a domain-specific data representation;
- a recognizable product object;
- a selection language;
- a transition or gesture;
- a material behavior;
- continuity across screens.

Require it to be product-specific, perceptible, repeatable, functional,
sustainable across screens, and accessible.

Do not call a gradient, larger radius, blur, color swap, generic animation, or
larger card a signature.

Reject the signature when it attracts attention without improving recognition,
continuity, decision, or state comprehension. Preserve a conventional path and
safe reduced-motion behavior.
