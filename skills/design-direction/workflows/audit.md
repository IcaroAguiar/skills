# Audit

Determine whether the rendered interface is styled, composed, authored,
refined, or premium.

Inspect:

- context specificity;
- visible thesis;
- dominant composition and attention path;
- semantic visual-system roles;
- task-specific component choices;
- assets and fallbacks;
- signature usefulness;
- state and multi-screen coherence;
- motion and feedback;
- platform adaptation;
- content resilience;
- design-direction revalidation;
- evidence supporting the claimed level.

Use [audit-report.md](../templates/audit-report.md). Treat an attractive
screenshot with unverified behavior as static craft evidence, not proof of
premium or native quality.

Score every supported axis with
[authorship-evaluation.md](../references/authorship-evaluation.md). Use the score
to expose strengths and weaknesses; apply limiters before classification.

## Complete when

- every audit axis has observed evidence or `N/V`;
- foundation failures constrain the verdict;
- the classification does not exceed the tested runtime and platforms;
- every finding in the audit-report template is complete.
