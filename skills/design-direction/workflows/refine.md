# Refine

Use when the governing thesis is already approved but the execution is
inconsistent, incomplete, generic in places, or disproportionate.

1. Load the approved [direction decision](../templates/direction-decision.md).
2. Capture comparable baseline evidence. Record classification and authorship scores when the requested deliverable includes those judgments.
3. Name the exact surfaces and states that under-execute the thesis.
4. Preserve task, context lock, composition thesis, approved attributes, and
   positive patterns.
5. Change only the smallest coherent set of typography, rhythm, material,
   components, assets, signature, motion, or state expression needed to restore
   the direction.
6. Render or implement the authorized refinement.
7. Revalidate the affected hard gates and `design-direction` invariants.
   Re-score under the same evidence conditions when scoring is in scope.
8. Show equivalent before/after evidence and ask whether to accept, refine, or
   revert when the workflow remains interactive.

Route to `explore` instead when the governing thesis itself is disputed. Route
to `design-direction` when a foundation fails.

## Complete when

- every change maps to a named inconsistency;
- the approved thesis and preserved attributes remain intact;
- authorship improves without a new foundation failure;
- before/after conditions are equivalent or their limits are explicit;
- the user decision is recorded when adoption remains open.
