# Translate Reference

Treat references as evidence of design functions, not a costume to copy.
Read [precedent-library.md](../references/precedent-library.md) and verify the
current artifact before treating a named product as evidence.

For every material element record:

```text
Observed element:
Function:
Proportion and hierarchy:
Rhythm:
Behavior:
Material:
Asset role:
Why it works there:
Context dependency:
Translation for this product:
What must not be copied:
```

Translate relationships before appearance. A dominant financial card may
translate into a focal clinical encounter, not a large green rectangle.

Reject the translation when branding, proprietary imagery, complete
composition, or an out-of-context control is being reproduced.

After extraction, route to `explore` when high-impact choices remain undecided,
to `refine` when an approved thesis already exists, or to `execute` when the
translated direction is sufficiently specified and authorized.

## Complete when

- every material reference element has a recorded function and dependency;
- proprietary or context-bound expression is excluded from the translation;
- each translated relationship maps to this product, task, and platform;
- the next mode and approval status are explicit.
